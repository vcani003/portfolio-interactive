# Chapter transporter

Decided 2026-09-26. Built the same day. Gameplay owns it. It is the existing chapter change plus a floor hold, not a new agent.

## What it is

Walking onto a pad on the right side of the floor carries you to the next chapter. It uses the same 650ms hold as entering from the timeline, then the scene crossfades. The Next chapter button stays, so you can still skip ahead without walking.

## The pad

Each playable chapter gets one floor pad on the right edge of the walkable ground, clear of the spawn point, the computer, and the campus cat’s path. Stepping onto it starts the green fill. Walking off before it finishes cancels it, the same way letting go of E cancels the timeline hold. Standing still through the fill is the commitment. A quick walk across does not skip the chapter.

While you stand on it, the label is the next chapter’s existing name:

- College → JPMorgan Chase
- JPMorgan Chase, including the internship closet → Fortress
- Fortress → Banh Miow Cafe
- Café → Game development

No new line of copy.

## The transition

When the fill completes, movement locks and the current scene fades into the next backdrop. You arrive at that chapter’s usual starting spot, which is away from its pad, so you do not immediately travel again. The song keeps playing.

On the last chapter, Game development, there is no pad. “Let’s talk” stays on the button, so the plaza does not close the journey by accident.

Reduced motion skips the fade and changes the chapter as soon as the hold completes.

## Also on the page

The chapter tabs and Previous chapter / Next chapter stay. The mute note sits at the top of the scene so it does not cover the pad.
